## Chosen Direction: Signal Noir

### Design Movement
Neo-noir editorial design fused with analog broadcast graphics and contemporary motion identity.

### Core Principles
1. Treat each video as a transmission with its own visual temperature, rather than a generic content card.
2. Use tension between near-black surfaces, electric coral signal marks, and quiet cream typography.
3. Prefer asymmetric compositions, editorial cropping, and layered depth over centered marketing blocks.
4. Let motion feel like a tuned signal: scanlines, slow parallax, waveform pulses, and quick broadcast cuts.

### Color Philosophy
The base is near-black graphite to give the site the feeling of a dark screening room. Warm paper-cream carries the reading experience, while signal coral marks current, energy, and human intent. Acid chartreuse is reserved for status moments and hover feedback, creating a precise sense of live transmission without resorting to generic neon gradients.

### Layout Paradigm
The page behaves like a vertical broadcast wall: a narrow utility rail, a large editorial hero, an offset featured reel, and a staggered archive of video transmissions. Content alternates between wide cinematic fields and tight metadata strips. The grid is intentionally uneven so the site feels art-directed rather than templated.

### Signature Elements
- A persistent signal rail with a live “ON AIR” indicator, timecode, and channel handle.
- Thin coral rules, index numbers, and broadcast labels such as TRANSMISSION 01.
- Grain, scanline, and subtle RGB-shift textures used sparingly to create analog depth.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions should feel like tuning a broadcast. Hovering a video shifts its image crop and reveals metadata; active controls snap with a short signal pulse; clicking a card opens the video in a focused viewing state. All interactions remain readable and keyboard accessible.

### Animation
Use slow 14–20 second ambient drift for background layers, 180–260ms ease-out transitions for controls, staggered entrance for cards, and short 80–140ms signal flickers for status accents. Keep video imagery mostly stable; motion should create anticipation, not compete with the content. Respect prefers-reduced-motion.

### Typography System
Use Bebas Neue or Oswald for signal labels and display headlines, paired with Source Sans 3 for body copy and metadata. Headlines use tight tracking and controlled line breaks; metadata uses uppercase, generous tracking, and small size. Never use Inter.

### Brand Essence
Foxy CodeName is an independent AI animation and music-video channel for viewers who want cinematic experiments, strange remixes, and emotionally charged visual worlds without algorithmic blandness.
Personality adjectives: cinematic, curious, electric.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are concise, atmospheric, and confident. CTAs sound like invitations into a screening room, not generic conversion copy.
Example lines: “ENTER THE TRANSMISSION.” / “PLAY THE STRANGE CUT.”

### Wordmark & Logo
A compact fox-ear signal mark: two angular ears framing a small broadcast dot, paired with a custom condensed wordmark that feels stamped from a late-night station ID. The mark should work as a favicon and as the icon in the signal rail.

### Signature Brand Color
Signal Coral — #FF5B4D.

## Style Decisions

- The AI Tools page is a live broadcast/tuning deck, using channel IDs, timecodes, indexed signals, and transmission-state language instead of generic dashboard language.
- Dark-page hero sections include a cinematic signal artifact such as a waveform field, scanline layer, or cropped transmission frame.
- Tool cards use indexed metadata, temperature/status cues, and editorial variation so they feel like instruments in a transmission deck rather than uniform SaaS cards.
